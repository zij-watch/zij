- 13 Nov 12:45, a satellite counts as flying from its first detection in the loaded countries (Terra 2 Nov 2000, Aqua 4 Jul 2002, Suomi NPP 20 Jan 2012, NOAA-20 1 Apr 2018) to 31 Dec 2025, known gaps left out. - 13 Nov 12:45, the weekly value of a sensor is its satellites' fire cell-days, divided by the satellite-days flown that week, times 7. A sensor-week with fewer than 3 satellite-days is "no data" and gets no row; a week the sensor flew on fewer than 7 days is flagged partial. Weeks are ISO weeks, starting Monday. Why: our spec, section 5.3. Adding the satellites up made VIIRS jump when NOAA-20 joined in 2018.
