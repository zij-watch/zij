- 13 Nov 12:44, the first build loads the FIRMS yearly country files for Egypt and Portugal, 2000 to 2025 (MODIS, VIIRS Suomi NPP, VIIRS NOAA-20). The 2026 API pulls and NOAA-21 wait. Why: the first checkpoint covers 2012 to 2024, and API rows need their own steps (clipping to the country outline, near-real-time rows have no `type`).
- 13 Nov 12:44, the static-source mask is every 0.01 degree cell with `type` 2 or 3 detections on 20 or more different days, counted over all satellites and all years; every detection in such a cell is dropped, in every year. Why: our spec, section 5.1. It removes gas flares and industry that slip through as `type` 0.
- 13 Nov 12:44, a detection gets one filter reason, checked in this order: `type` (only 0 is kept), then confidence (MODIS 30 and above, VIIRS nominal and high), then the static-source mask. Why: the kept rows are the same in any order; the order only decides which reason a doubly-rejected row is filed under.
- 13 Nov 12:45, coordinates are turned into whole 1e-5 degree units before snapping to the 0.01 and 0.1 degree grids. Why: plain floating point puts a point like 30.07 in the wrong cell.
- 13 Nov 12:45, satellite outages come from one list of 31 known gaps, read from our pre-event spec (section 4), which verified them against four regions and NASA's outage notices. A gap day is "no data" for that satellite, never zero. Why: an outage hits the whole instrument, and a quiet week must not be read as one.
- 13 Nov 12:45, a satellite counts as flying from its first detection in the loaded countries (Terra 2 Nov 2000, Aqua 4 Jul 2002, Suomi NPP 20 Jan 2012, NOAA-20 1 Apr 2018) to 31 Dec 2025, known gaps left out. Why: the spec gives no start dates. To confirm: our research seems to have used each country's own first detection (see the checkpoint line).
- 13 Nov 12:45, the weekly value of a sensor is its satellites' fire cell-days, divided by the satellite-days flown that week, times 7. A sensor-week with fewer than 3 satellite-days is "no data" and gets no row; a week the sensor flew on fewer than 7 days is flagged partial. Weeks are ISO weeks, starting Monday. Why: our spec, section 5.3. Adding the satellites up made VIIRS jump when NOAA-20 joined in 2018.

Own fit from 100 MODIS fire weeks in 2012 to 2020, blend from 30 to 99 with weight fire weeks / 100, nothing below 30. Why: spec 5.5c.
- 13 Nov 12:51, parameter from pre-event research: fit on 2012 to 2020, test out of time on 2021 to 2025. b grid 0.20 to 1.60 in 71 steps of 0.02. g grid 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5. Why: spec 5.5; Aqua's overpass drifts later after 2020.
- 13 Nov 12:51, parameter from pre-event research: the ridge on c[month] has strength 2.0 and pulls each month towards the single background c of V = c + a M^b fitted at the same b. Negative c and a are set to 0, and predictions below 0 are set to 0. Why: a month with few weeks should not get a background of its own from noise.
- 13 Nov 12:51, parameter from pre-event research: the fire-size term z is the log of MODIS mean FRP per detection that week, minus the median of that log over the training weeks with MODIS fires, kept within -2 to 2, and 0 in a week with no MODIS detection.
- 13 Nov 12:51, parameter from pre-event research: an area's season is the 3 calendar months in a row (December can run into January) with the highest mean weekly VIIRS fire days in 2012 to 2020. In M4, r is the year's MODIS total over those months divided by the typical one; typical is the median of the training years' totals; r is kept within 0.02 to 10, and is 1 for a year with no season weeks.
- 13 Nov 12:51, parameter from pre-event research: the single-satellite ratio is (Terra + Aqua average) / (one satellite alone) by calendar month, on 2012 to 2020 weeks when both flew all 7 days, each month pulled to the area's overall ratio by 20 cell-days. It is applied before M4 only, as in the research numbers we compare with.
- 13 Nov 12:51, parameter from pre-event research: a week belongs to the ISO year of its Monday and the month of its Thursday. The 8-area season error is the mean over every area and year; an area's Spearman is the median over its years.
- 13 Nov 12:51, weeks when either sensor flew fewer than 7 days are left out of both fitting and scoring (Mohanad). Why: a partial week is not a fair count. Our research kept them in scoring; in 2012 to 2025 that is one week, in January 2012.
