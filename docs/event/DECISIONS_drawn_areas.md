# Decisions: drawn areas

Lines for `DECISIONS.md`, written on the `feat/drawn-areas` branch so the two never conflict. When the branch is merged, move these lines to the bottom of `DECISIONS.md` as they are and delete this file. Same rules: time (Cairo), decision, why; a line is never edited afterwards.

- 13 Nov 13:55, drawn areas get their own files, `app/public/data/cells/`: per 0.1 degree cell, ISO week and satellite, the fire cell-days, the kept detections and (MODIS) the FRP sum, as one gzipped binary file per country with `index.json` beside it. Egypt 0.18 MB, Portugal 0.15 MB, Argentina 4.23 MB (11 MB unzipped). Why: with these the browser can do for any box what the pipeline does for a named area. They are counts per cell and week, not FIRMS rows. Format: `docs/event/CELLS_FORMAT.md`.
- 13 Nov 13:55, the country's M4 fit travels in `cells/index.json`, once on every training year and once with each training year left out, with the country's MODIS training total. Why: a blended area needs the country's fit, and the leave-one-year-out bands need it without each year; fitting a country in the browser would mean loading the whole country first.
- 13 Nov 13:55, the M4 fit and the small-area rule are ported to TypeScript (`app/lib/fit/`), with no library: the normal equations are solved by Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting. Checked against the pipeline on 22 areas and boxes (13 own fits, 6 blends, 3 with no fit): `b` and `g` are identical, the other parameters differ by 2e-12 at most and the predictions by 7e-13.
- 13 Nov 13:55, the random boxes of our research are drawn new. Our notes describe them (10 boxes of 0.3 degree; 83 boxes of 0.2 to 0.8 degree) and give no coordinates, so these are placed around a random cell that had a MODIS fire in 2012 to 2020, with seed 20261113: 83 in Egypt and Portugal, 83 more in Argentina. Why: the numbers can be compared with the research ones, but they are not a rerun of them. Tables: `docs/event/SMALL_AREAS.md`.
- 13 Nov 13:55, small areas checked on the 83 boxes in Egypt and Portugal. Median season error, leave one year out: 12% with an own fit (research: 10 to 16%), 25% with the blend (25 to 39%), 41% under 30 fire weeks (49% or worse).
- 13 Nov 14:05, decided (Mohanad): the season band of a blended area is -54% to +63%, measured on the blended boxes of the three countries (459 box-years). It replaces the research band of -47% to +60%. Egypt and Portugal alone give -53% to +56% (270 box-years). Why: a band measured at the event on the data in use, with Argentina in it, since drawn areas can be in Argentina.
- 13 Nov 14:05, decided (Mohanad): the small-area rule stays as it is, although in these boxes the blend was no better than the area's own fit (median season error 26% for both over the blended boxes of the three countries). Our research found the blend better than either fit alone. Why: the rule is the researched one, and one set of random boxes is not enough to change it. To confirm after the event.
- 13 Nov 14:05, decided (Mohanad): Argentina's 4.2 MB cell file is committed. The app loads a country's file lazily, only when the user's area is in that country. Why: the deployed app needs it, the tests read it, and nobody who stays on the presets pays for it.
- 13 Nov 14:11, a drawn area gets everything a preset gets, computed in the browser (`app/lib/fit/panel.ts`): months and seasons, the month x year table, the typical year, this month against the median, the critical period, anomalies, the month band and the Terra-only season band. The document has the keys, units and rounding of a preset file. Checked against the 10 exported area files and the pipeline's documents for 6 random boxes (3 blended, 3 with no fit): 425,523 numbers, all identical.
- 13 Nov 14:11, numbers in a drawn area's document are rounded as Python rounds them: to the nearest, and an exact half to the even digit. Why: JavaScript's own rounding sends halves up, which would differ from the exported files on values like 0.125.
- 13 Nov 14:11, in the browser a value that sits on its percentile is not flagged as an anomaly: a flag needs the value outside the 10th to 90th percentile by more than one part in a billion. Why: a month is often one of its own baseline years, so its value can be the percentile itself, and two sums that differ in the 15th decimal must not decide a flag. It changed 2 flags out of about 16,000 and made all of them match the pipeline's.
- 13 Nov 14:11, a drawn area can be a polygon. A cell is in it when its centre is, as for a box; holes are left out. The document of a polygon has one more key, `polygon`, and its `box` is the box around it; `n_cells` counts the grid cells whose centre is inside.
- 13 Nov 14:11, the "number of fires detected" view reads kept detections per sensor and per satellite from the cell files, for the weeks that touch the date range: the range is widened to whole ISO weeks and the widened range is shown. Why: the cell files are weekly. Counts for single days need the day view's data. A week when a sensor has no data is "no data", never zero. These counts are never averaged over satellites and never harmonized.
- 13 Nov 14:11, the basis text of a blended area's band is now "pooled small-area band (30 to 99 MODIS fire weeks)", without "from pre-event research". Why: the band is measured at the event now. No named area is blended, so no exported file changes.
